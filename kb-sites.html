About

Web Design Agency

KB Sites is an independent web studio that creates custom websites for local service-based businesses worldwide. You deal directly with the person designing and developing your site — no account managers, no waiting in line, and no monthly lock-in contracts. Websites delivered in days, not months.

Each site is tailor-made for your business — featuring your services, your colors, and your actual photos; no ready-made templates. Our pricing is simple and upfront because we prefer doing excellent work for local entrepreneurs over inflating the bill.
