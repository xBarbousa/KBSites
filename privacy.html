Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains what personal information KB Sites collects when you create an account, contact us, order a website or join our Partner Program, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have.

1.Who is responsible

Data Controller: Kauã Barbosa dos Santos, operating as KB Sites — an independent web design studio based in Brazil — is responsible for the personal information described here. For any privacy question or request, send us a message through the support chat in your account or the contact form on our website (see "Your rights" below). These are also our channels for communication with data subjects.

2.Information we collect

  • Account information: an account is required to request a project. When you create one we collect your name, email address and password — the password is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we cannot read — plus a profile photo if you add one. If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account ID with us.
  • Information you give us through our request form, the support chat or by email: your name, business name, email address, your message, the plan you choose, and any project materials or links you decide to send (such as your current website, your Google Business profile, photos, files or references).
  • Partner Program information: if you join as a partner, your referral code, the referrals credited to you, your commission records, and the payout details you give us (for example a PayPal email or bank / Pix details).
  • Payment information: payments are handled by PayPal. We do not collect or store your card or bank details — PayPal does, under its own privacy policy.
  • Security & anti-fraud data: when you use your account or send us a request, we record your IP address and a random device identifier kept in a first-party cookie (kb_dev, see section 7), together with the dates they were first and last seen. We link these to your account and to the requests you send.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our host may keep standard server logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) for security and operation.

3.How we use your information

  • To run your account and your private project chat with us.
  • To reply to you, prepare a quote, and design, deliver, host and maintain your website, depending on your plan.
  • To communicate about your project, invoices and support, including the emails described in section 4.
  • To keep accounts secure and to prevent fraud — for example, to check that a partner is not earning commission on their own purchase (a "self-referral").
  • To credit referrals and pay partner commissions.
  • To keep records and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

4.Emails we send

Your request, our replies and project updates are kept in the private chat in your account. We also email you about them. These are service (transactional) emails, not marketing, and they go to the email address on your account:

  • Account emails — such as the 6-digit code that confirms your email address when you sign up.
  • Request confirmation — when you send us a request, to confirm we received it and which plan you chose, with a link to your chat.
  • Status updates — when your project moves forward, for example when your proposal is ready, your payment is confirmed or your site goes live.
  • Replies — when we answer you in your chat.

Other notifications — such as a welcome message, a security notice when your password changes, or Partner Program updates (new referrals, commissions earned or paid, rate changes) — are optional and switched off by default. If we turn any of them on and you'd rather not receive them, tell us in your account chat and we'll stop sending them to you. We keep a simple log of the emails we send (type, recipient, subject, date and whether sending succeeded) so we can make sure they work.

5.Legal basis

Where laws such as the Brazilian LGPD or the EU/UK GDPR apply, we process your data to take steps at your request and to perform our contract with you (including running your account and sending the service emails above), to comply with legal obligations, and based on our legitimate interest in running the studio and answering enquiries. We record IP addresses and device identifiers based on our legitimate interest in keeping accounts secure and preventing fraud, including in our Partner Program. We do not rely on consent for most of this processing. Where we do rely on your consent for a specific purpose, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

6.Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with service providers that help us operate, each under their own terms:

  • Our email & hosting provider — when you create an account or send a request, the information is saved on our own hosting, and the emails described in section 4 (and our own notifications, so we can reply) are sent through its mail servers.
  • PayPal — processes payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider — stores and serves the website and may keep standard server logs.
  • Google — if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google confirms who you are and shares the details listed in section 2, under Google's own privacy policy. If your profile photo comes from Google, it may be loaded from Google's servers when it's shown.
  • Google Fonts — serves the fonts; when a page loads, Google may receive your IP address as part of delivering the fonts.

If you reached us through a partner's referral link, that partner can see in their dashboard your business name (or your name, if you didn't give one), the date of your request and whether it became a paid project. Partners never see your email address, your messages or your other details.

Some of these providers are located outside your country, so your information may be processed abroad (see "International transfers" below). We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not otherwise share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

7.Cookies & local storage

We use a small number of first-party cookies and browser storage items, and no advertising or third-party analytics trackers:

  • Referral cookie (kb_ref) — if you arrive through a partner's referral link, we store that partner's code for up to 30 days so the referral can be credited if you become a client. It contains only the partner code, not personal data.
  • Login session cookie — a standard session cookie that keeps you signed in and protects our forms while you use your account or send a request. It is deleted when you close your browser.
  • "Keep me signed in" cookie (kb_remember) — set when you sign in, so you stay signed in for up to 60 days, even after closing your browser. It holds only a random token (we store a scrambled copy of it) and is removed when you log out.
  • Device identifier cookie (kb_dev) — a random code that lets us recognise the same browser again, used only to secure accounts and to detect Partner Program fraud (see sections 2 and 5). It lasts one year and is never used for advertising or to follow you across other websites.
  • Hosting security cookie — our hosting provider's bot protection may set a short cookie (named humans_ followed by a number) to confirm that a real person is sending a form.
  • Local storage — we remember that you dismissed the cookie notice, and your referral code, on your own device.

External resources we load (Google Fonts) and services you actively use (such as PayPal or Google sign-in) are governed by their own policies and may set their own cookies when you interact with them.

8.How long we keep it

  • Enquiry and project information (including your chat, and the IP address and device identifier recorded with each request) — for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, accounting and record-keeping requirements, and then deleted or anonymised.
  • Account information — for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see below).
  • Security & anti-fraud records (IP addresses and device identifiers linked to your account) — while your account is in use, and deleted or anonymised no later than 24 months after your last activity. Records connected to a commission that is under review or in dispute may be kept until the matter is closed.
  • Email log — up to 12 months.
  • Partner and commission records — for as long as needed for accounting and to settle any payout questions.

You can ask us to delete your data sooner (see below).

9.Your rights

Depending on applicable law (for example the LGPD, GDPR, or U.S. state laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights including: access; confirmation that we process your data; correction; deletion; portability (a copy of your data); information about how your data is shared; objection to or restriction of certain processing; anonymisation or blocking in certain cases; and withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis.

To exercise any of these — including asking for a copy of your data or for your account and data to be deleted — send us a message through the support chat in your account (kbsites.com.br/account) or the contact form on our website. If you can't sign in, you can also reply to any email you've received from us. We may ask you to confirm that the request really comes from you (for example, from the email address on your account) before acting on it, and we will respond within a reasonable time. When we delete an account, we may keep what the law requires or what is needed to prevent fraud (such as payment and commission records), and nothing more.

10.International transfers

KB Sites operates from Brazil and serves clients in other countries, including the United States. This means your information may be processed in, or transferred to, countries other than your own. We take reasonable steps to protect it wherever it is processed.

11.Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information — for example, encrypted (HTTPS) connections, passwords stored only in hashed form, and the account and device checks described above. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12.Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is the one published here, shown by the "Last updated" date above.

14.Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Send us a message through the support chat in your account (kbsites.com.br/account) or the contact form on our website. You can also reply to any email you've received from us.
